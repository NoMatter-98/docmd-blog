happiness is a butterfly
sometimes it gets so close
sometimes it flies away high

adults prefer to breeding in the cage
children wanna catch it from the sky
but both can not prevent
finally lost and death

oh my dear friend
hope you can cherish it by love
deeply inside your heart
enjoy it when you meet
treasure it during your life

——XDM.06/2023

Notes：前段时间随手写的诗歌了，今天再看忽然觉得确实是有意义的，对于离别或者失去的态度该怎样才好？倒也不是说“反正都这样无所谓啦”，而是每次拥有时都是珍惜的态度，那么得失心就没那么重，心态会轻松一些吧。
