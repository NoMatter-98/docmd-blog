Moon lights tonight
Mood slides to be bright
Counting the stars
Finding the pair
Seeing your hair
Flying over the sky

wanna know whether you can see
the same stars at the same time

One two love
Want to love

I dont want to be
once to love

one two love
want to love

Cause i know that
this is the only chance

time sets the gap
even light can not break
it takes years
to bring them together

even light the faster
has no narrow escape

but count our stars
at this moment
we meet just in seconds

count with me

One two love
Want to love

I dont want to be
once to love

one two love
want to love

Cause i know that
you are just the only one

创作力源于不想干正事儿但是不得不干点什么事儿的时候

——薛丁墨 2023.6.1
